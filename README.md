A dense newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in one image: 48 stories first reported that day, across 8 regions and 9 fields, with region and field count bars counted from the stories themselves and a by-the-numbers strip ($81.3B for the record Anthropic IPO, 13 years of waiting ended by GTA VI, $1.5BN in GTA VI pre-orders, 980M Anthropic shares sold, 60/4 for New Zealand at lunch in Wellington, 150,000 tents stuck at the Gaza border). Thirteen illustrated lead cards with detailed flat-vector scenes (Vice City at sunset for the GTA VI rollout, the three flags over the Shenzhen trilateral, the rising chart and bell for Anthropic's debut, the Crimean Bridge under its third strike, the Peace Palace for the ICJ verdict, the spiral of Cyclone Akvan, the oil derrick for the Venezuelan $12bn report, the Knesset for the Eisenkot mandate, the aid convoy and tents for Gaza, the Estonian border fence, the Spanish strike pickets, the Wellington pitch for Bumrah's first-over wicket, the theatre stage for Stacy Keach), a dotted world map with numbered pins for every lead, 35 one-line briefs, and a date-check footer that names the big 18 November stories left out on purpose. Every headline and figure was checked against reporting of the day, and the big stories first reported on 18 November were kept out: the Van Gogh $190.4m sale, the Jeffries nomination, the Fed minutes, the EU €45bn loan, the Gaza Yellow Line raid, the Voyager 1 light-day crossing, and Duckett's 133. The program draws the whole page itself with a hand-built bitmap font, bold-struck so the headlines read at small sizes, and its own PNG encoder, using only the Python standard library, and produces byte-identical output on every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
