A dense newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in one image: 42 stories reported that day, across 8 regions and 9 fields, with region and field count bars counted from the stories themselves and a by-the-numbers strip ($190.4M for the record Van Gogh, $1.4T for Anthropic's debut valuation, €45B for the EU loan proposed for Kyiv, 13 years of waiting ended by GTA VI, 1 light-day for Voyager 1). Thirteen illustrated lead cards with detailed flat-vector scenes (Vice City at sunset for the GTA VI rollout, the three flags over the Shenzhen trilateral, the blossom tree in its gold frame for the Van Gogh record, the rising chart and bell for Anthropic's debut, the Crimean Bridge, the Gaza ground raid, Cyclone Akvan, the Peace Palace, Voyager 1 one light-day out, the Capitol dome, the Fed's rising arrow, the North Korean missile launch, Bumrah's first-over wicket at Wellington), a dotted world map with numbered pins for every lead, 29 one-line briefs, and a date-check footer. Every headline and figure was checked against reporting of the day: the Van Gogh sale, the Anthropic first trade, the GTA VI launch, the Fed minutes, the Crimea attack, the Gaza raid, the Akvan landfall track, the ICJ verdict, the Voyager milestone, the Jeffries nomination, the North Korean launches, and the Wellington Test all broke on 19 November. The program draws the whole page itself with a hand-built bitmap font and its own PNG encoder, using only the Python standard library, and produces byte-identical output on every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
